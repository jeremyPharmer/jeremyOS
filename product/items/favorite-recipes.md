## Problem

Jeremy wants a **favorite recipes** section in JeremyOS. There is no personal place to keep and reopen recipes he actually cooks.

## Outcome

Jeremy can save and open a short list of favorite recipes (title + link or short notes) from JeremyOS.

## Scope (v1)

- Favorites list: title, optional URL, optional short notes
- Add / edit / remove; ordered by Jeremy (or recency)
- Thin section — not a recipe-network or meal-planner

## Out of scope / later

- Full recipe editor with ingredients/steps CMS
- Grocery lists, meal planning calendar, nutrition macros
- Scraping arbitrary recipe sites (link-out is enough for v1)

## Dependencies & risks

- Keep separate from recovery content catalog (RB-005) — this is personal kitchen, not recovery offers
- Open: paste-link only vs light in-app notes body
